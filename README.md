One line, at most 200 characters: the self-checkout is judging you.

Entry: `The self-checkout scanned my kale, paused, and printed 'unexpected item in bagging area' — which is also what my therapist said about my life choices.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
